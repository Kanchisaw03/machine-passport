# Machine Passport pilot sheet

## Before the conversation

- Ask permission to show the prototype and to take notes. Do not record a person's voice or collect a name unless they specifically agree and it is needed.
- Use the SEEDED guided walkthrough unless the participant offers a safe machine and recording location. Never present public sample clips as their machine data.
- Do not ask the participant to bypass guarding or perform work on energized equipment. Follow the site's safety procedures.

## Five-minute conversation

1. Open **Guided demo**. Explain that bundled recordings are labeled SEEDED and the recommendation may be a deterministic template when Gemini is unavailable.
2. Run the sample loop; show the baseline, healthy/faulty sample checks, seeded maintenance note, evidence IDs, provider label, and approval control.
3. If a participant supplies a real recording, create a separate machine and keep its placement and provenance clear. Do not replace the SEEDED demo baseline with mixed-source clips.
4. Invite them to submit the in-app usefulness response. Explain that feedback is stored with an optional role and free-text suggestion; ask them not to include personal or confidential information.
5. Ask these questions without suggesting a preferred answer:
   - What part of the workflow was unclear?
   - Would you trust the distinction between measured data and sample data? Why?
   - What evidence would you need before acting on a recommendation?
   - Which step would slow you down during a real inspection?
   - What should the system show when it has insufficient evidence?

## Logging answers honestly

Record the date, participant role only if volunteered, which screens were shown, exact question asked, and a concise paraphrase of the answer. Keep direct quotations only with explicit permission. Separate observed behavior from interpretation. Note problems and negative feedback alongside positive comments. Do not write testimonials or infer customer demand from a single conversation.
